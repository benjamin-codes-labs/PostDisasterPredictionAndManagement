使用範例Usage Examples

識別垃圾並標註位置Recognize garbage & mark location

  1. 點擊"開啟攝像頭"，將垃圾置於鏡頭中央。Click 'Start Camera' and center the garbage in view.
  2. 點擊"拍攝識別"，等待後台返回識別結果。Click 'Recognize' and wait for the detection result.
  3. 若識別到目標，系統會在當前地圖中心添加標註。If detected, a marker is added at the current map center.
  4. 可以匯出 CSV 用於統計或救援分配。Export the CSV for reporting or rescue allocation.

查看城市熱力與快速定位View city heatmaps & quick center

  1. 在"城市地圖預覽"中查看各城市分佈。Check the 'City Map Preview' for distributions.
  2. （建議）點擊某城市地圖並使用地圖控制項將主地圖移動到該城市進行細緻查看。(Recommended) Click a city map and center the main map there for details.
  3. 使用熱力圖切換查看高密度區域（需上傳足夠資料）。Use heatmap toggle to see hotspots (requires sufficient data).

災害應對要點 / Disaster Response Tips

地震Earthquake

  • 就地避難：蹲下、掩護、穩住（掉落物防護）Drop, Cover and Hold On: protect from falling debris
  • 遠離窗戶和高層傢具Stay away from windows and tall furniture
  • 建築物外需遠離外牆與電線Outside: keep clear of exterior walls and power lines
  • 備好應急包（水、藥品、手電、口罩）Prepare an emergency kit (water, meds, torch, masks)

洪水/水浸Flood

  • 迅速轉移到高處，切勿涉水行走Move to higher ground quickly; do not walk through floodwater
  • 關閉燃氣與電源（若安全可操作）Turn off gas and electricity if safe to do so
  • 聽從當地政府疏散指引Follow local evacuation orders
  • 備足飲用水與重要文件防水保護Keep drinking water and waterproof important documents

颱風/颶風Typhoon / Hurricane

  • 加固門窗，移至房屋內側安全處Secure doors/windows and shelter in interior room
  • 避開易墜物區域與低窪地帶Avoid areas with falling objects and low-lying zones
  • 準備充足糧水與應急照明Prepare food, water and emergency lighting

野火Wildfire

  • 遵守疏散令，盡早撤離Evacuate early when ordered
  • 用濕布捂住口鼻並避免吸入煙霧Cover mouth/nose with wet cloth to avoid smoke inhalation
  • 關閉門窗，阻止火星進入室內Close windows/doors to prevent embers entering

極端高溫/熱浪Extreme Heat / Heatwave

  • 多飲水、避免中午高溫時段外出Drink plenty of water; avoid outdoor activity during peak heat
  • 為弱勢群體檢查降溫與通風情況Check cooling and ventilation for vulnerable people
  • 識別中暑症狀並及時就醫Recognize heatstroke signs and seek medical care

傳染病與疫情Infectious Disease / Pandemic

  • 勤洗手、佩戴口罩，遵循公共衛生指引Wash hands, wear masks, follow public health guidance
  • 盡量保持社交距離並接種推薦疫苗Maintain distancing and get recommended vaccines
  • 如有症狀及時隔離並就醫Isolate and seek care if symptomatic
